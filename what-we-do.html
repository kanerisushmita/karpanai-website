What We Do

We don't just teach Social Science.
We make it come alive.

At Karpanai, art becomes a bridge between the classroom and the world outside it.

🎭 Flagship programme

Civic Imagination Studios

What if your classroom became a theatre, newsroom, parliament, studio and storytelling space? Our flagship programme transforms classrooms into art-integrated Social Science learning environments.

Students explore:

History through theatre Geography through music & songwriting Civics through debate & roleplay Social issues through storytelling Identity through writing Public issues through open conversation

The goal isn't to make every child an artist. It is to help every child find a way to express what they think.

🌱 Where it all began

Project Dhairiyam

A pilot with 74 government school students using theatre, storytelling, open mics and creative arts to build confidence, expression and self-belief.

Dhairiyam became the foundation upon which Karpanai's larger approach to art-integrated learning evolved.

74 students → countless questions → one bigger idea

🎤 Student showcase

Thulir — Student Showcase

Learning deserves an audience.

Thulir creates a public platform where students can share what they have learned and created. The showcase brings together:

🎭 Theatre 🎤 Open Mic 📚 Book Launches 🎨 Creative Expression 💬 Student Voices

Parents, educators, community members and audiences get to witness something important: children are not just receiving knowledge. They are creating it.

📚 From page to publication

Young Authors Programme

From classroom thoughts to published books.

The Young Authors Programme enabled 16 students aged 12–14 to become published authors. Students explored themes including:

Discrimination Identity Equality Civic awareness Local history Belonging

Because sometimes the best way to understand an issue is to write your own story about it.
